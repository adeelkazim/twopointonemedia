Build on what is already there

Your website already offers consultation calls to action and Mindbody booking. The opportunity is to connect content, campaigns and that existing journey more intentionally. Any recommendations for usability, speed or booking refinement would be validated through an audit—not assumed defects.

01 / The proposed strategy

One presence.
Six connected workstreams.

Not more noise. A consistent way to communicate your clinic’s expertise, support informed decisions and understand what is working.

01

Social media
management

A considered monthly editorial plan, branded captions and a clear publishing and approval workflow. Community response guidelines keep the voice consistent; sensitive clinical questions go to the clinic.Proposed output · Editorial calendar, publishing workflow, response guidance. Cadence to be agreed.

02

Content
creation

Planned content days for vertical video, consultation FAQs, treatment explainers and introductions to your people and space. The clinic approves clinical accuracy. Patient images and testimonials require explicit written consent; individual results are never promised.Proposed output · Shoot plan, edited content and clinic approval process.

03

Paid campaign
management

Platform-appropriate awareness and search campaigns, creative variants and landing-page tests in clinic-owned ad accounts. No sensitive health targeting or unconditional results claims. Launch is subject to platform policy and clinical review.Proposed output · Campaign plan, creative tests and reporting. Media budget separate from management fees.

04

Website
upgrade

Review the existing mobile consultation journey, treatment information hierarchy and booking clarity. Propose coherent campaign landing pages and test accessibility and speed. Preserve what works; prioritize changes using audit evidence.Proposed output · Prioritized audit and agreed page refinements—not an assumed full rebuild.

05

Search engine
optimization

Align Google Business Profile information, useful service and location content, and technical on-page foundations. Develop a legitimate review-request process, without fake reviews, keyword spam or ranking guarantees.Proposed output · Local search checklist and prioritized content and technical improvements.

06

Analytics
& learning

A consent-aware event plan for click-to-call, booking-link clicks and enquiry completion where appropriate, with campaign UTMs and a clear monthly report. No patient health data or diagnoses in analytics or ad platforms. Qualified consultations are measured only if the clinic can do so with consent; attribution is not guaranteed.Proposed output · Measurement plan, consent checks and an agreed reporting framework.

02 / Content with intention

Help people understand.
Then help them take the next step.

Proposed editorial themes—not approved medical advertising. Each piece has a purpose before it has a posting date.

  • Explain the treatment EDUCATION
  • Meet the people INTRODUCTIONS
  • Prepare for your visit GUIDANCE
  • Care and FAQs CLARITY
  • Clinic atmosphere FAMILIARITY
The creative principle

Calm, clear and
clinically considered.

Your sage, forest and warm-cream palette, refined typography and a consistent visual rhythm—adapted across social content and the website.

Accuracy before attention. Clinic approval before publication. Consent before patient imagery.

03 / An indicative first 90 days

Align. Create. Refine.

A proposed sequence, not a contracted schedule. Kickoff, dates, posting cadence and deliverables depend on agreed scope and approvals.

Phase 01 · Indicative days 1–30

Discover & align

Review existing channels and the booking journey. Agree audience, content priorities, approval owners, consent requirements and the measurement plan.

Phase 02 · Indicative days 31–60

Create & launch

Produce the first agreed content batch, refine priority website pages and prepare campaigns. Launch only after clinical, platform and consent checks.

Phase 03 · Indicative days 61–90

Learn & refine

Review permitted event data and content response. Refine creative and landing pages, then agree the next cycle with the clinic.

Consultation volume, treatment outcomes and advertising performance are not guaranteed.
