Coco Candy: Bringing Artisan Treats to Life

Photoshoot April 22, 2025

At Two Point One Media, we believe that every product has a story to tell—especially when it's handcrafted with passion and tradition. That's why we were thrilled to collaborate with Coco Candy, a brand that creates delicious artisan treats made in small batches. From stirring fresh coconut, peanuts, and tamarind to carefully hand-packaging each product, Coco Candy embodies craftsmanship and quality.

Elevating the Brand Through Professional Photography

When Coco Candy's founder approached us, she wanted to take her product presentation to the next level. Up until that point, her product photos had been captured on a phone, which didn't quite do justice to the intricate textures and vibrant colors of her treats. Our goal was to create clean, high-quality images that would enhance her brand's appeal and make her products truly stand out.

1. A Simple Yet Effective Setup

We opted for a minimalist approach using a white background and a controlled lighting setup to ensure the products remained the focal point. This setup allowed us to highlight the rich textures and colors of the coconut, peanuts, and tamarind in each treat, making them look as mouthwatering as they taste.

2. Capturing the Essence of Coco Candy

Rather than just taking standard product shots, we focused on showcasing the artisanal nature of Coco Candy. The goal was to make the treats feel authentic, fresh, and inviting, giving customers a real sense of the craftsmanship behind every bite.

3. Enhancing the Digital Presence

Beyond the photoshoot, we also updated and enhanced Coco Candy's website with the new product shots. These high-resolution images now serve as a key visual element on the website, making the online shopping experience more engaging and appealing to potential customers.

Why Professional Product Photography Matters

In the world of online retail, first impressions are everything. High-quality product photography helps build trust with customers, conveys a sense of quality, and ultimately drives more sales. With the new images, Coco Candy now has a polished and professional look that reflects the care and craftsmanship that goes into each of their treats.

At Two Point One Media, we specialize in bringing brands to life through expert photography and digital content. If your business is looking to elevate its product imagery, let's connect! We'd love to help you showcase your brand in the best possible light.
