How 2.1 Media Elevates B.I.G. Oakville's Social Media Presence

Insurance April 4, 2025

At Two Point One Media, we specialize in creating high-quality, engaging video content for businesses looking to strengthen their online presence. One of our standout collaborations is with B.I.G. Oakville—short for Billyard Insurance Group (Oakville Branch). Our partnership with B.I.G. Oakville has been a dynamic and rewarding experience, allowing us to bring valuable insurance knowledge to their audience in an entertaining and digestible way.

A Strategic Approach to Content Creation

Crafting effective social media content requires more than just filming and editing; it involves strategic planning, creativity, and continuous optimization. Our process with B.I.G. Oakville follows a structured yet flexible workflow to ensure their content remains relevant and impactful.

1. Bi-Weekly Brainstorming Sessions

Every other week, we meet with the B.I.G. Oakville team to brainstorm video ideas that align with their brand and engage their audience. These sessions allow us to stay ahead of industry trends, address common insurance questions, and develop creative ways to present information in an entertaining format.

2. Scriptwriting & Shot Planning

Once we identify compelling topics, we craft well-structured scripts that balance education with entertainment. This phase also includes meticulous shot planning to ensure smooth execution on shoot days, maximizing efficiency while maintaining high production value.

3. Video Shoot Days

On designated shoot days, our team works closely with B.I.G. Oakville to bring concepts to life. Whether it's breaking down complex insurance policies, busting common myths, or adding a touch of humor to industry topics, we focus on making the content both informative and engaging.

4. Editing & Client Revisions

After filming, our post-production team polishes the footage into high-quality videos that align with B.I.G. Oakville's branding. We collaborate with their team for feedback and revisions, ensuring each video meets their expectations before going live.

5. Posting & Engagement

Timing is everything when it comes to social media. We post B.I.G. Oakville's videos during optimal time slots to maximize reach and engagement. Beyond posting, we monitor analytics to assess performance and respond to any customer inquiries or leads generated from the content.

The Impact of Video Content for B.I.G. Oakville

Since implementing this strategic video content approach, B.I.G. Oakville has experienced increased engagement, improved audience interaction, and a stronger brand presence online. By consistently delivering valuable and entertaining content, we help position them as a trusted authority in the insurance industry while making insurance-related topics more accessible to their audience.

At Two Point One Media, we believe that storytelling is the key to connecting with audiences. Our partnership with B.I.G. Oakville exemplifies how well-crafted video content can elevate a brand's digital presence and drive meaningful engagement.

If your business is looking to harness the power of video marketing, let's connect! We're here to help you create compelling content that resonates with your audience and grows your brand.
